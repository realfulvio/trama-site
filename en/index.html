Where Trama works differently

Each point has a check you can open. Coverage is still partial, and we say so below.

  • Free, no licences to manage

    No cost, subscription or seat count. MIT licence: use it, study it and modify it, including in professional work.

    Check: MIT licence

  • It just starts

    Extract the ZIP folder and run Trama.exe. No Python to install, no account, no activation key.

    Check: getting started

  • Your data stays on your computer

    The application source imports no network module: no telemetry, no sync, no online licence check.

    Check: search urllib and socket

  • A readable project file

    In the simple case a .alab project is JSON text: open it in any editor and compare it with a diff.

    Check: sample file

  • Method and limits written into the result

    An exact test says when it switched to an approximation and why. An interval that cannot be computed is explained, not filled in.

    Check: feature map

  • Data, analyses and graphs stay linked

    Edit a value and the linked analyses recalculate. An excluded cell stays in the sheet and is left out of the calculation.

    Check: English README

What you can do

Six families of tools from the same worksheet.

Compare experiments

t-tests (also lognormal and TOST equivalence), exact rank tests, ANOVA, repeated measures, REML mixed models and multiple comparisons.

Fit and compare curves

118 catalogue equations, custom and conditional equations, global fits, weighting, AICc comparison, calibration, splines and LOWESS.

Explore complex data

PCA with biplot, K-means with reports for each k, hierarchical clustering with dendrograms, correlation matrices and heat maps.

Study outcomes and associations

Kaplan–Meier, Cox and competing risks, ROC curves with DeLong comparison, Bland–Altman, Passing–Bablok, multiple and logistic regression.

Prepare observations

In-sheet formulas, transformations, normalisation, area under the curve, data linked to CSV and Monte Carlo studies with a reproducible seed.

Compose the figure

XY and column graphs, box and violin plots, forest and bubble plots, annotations, layouts up to 12 panels and SVG or PDF export.

What Trama is not (yet)

Better to know before you choose.

  • It is a beta. 0.x versions can contain errors and incomplete features; there is no stable release yet.
  • Partial coverage. We do not claim equivalence with other programs: an established package may offer options, formats, support and years of maturity that Trama does not have.
  • Windows 10/11 64-bit only for the portable package.
  • No clinical validation or regulatory certification. Tests prove the cases that were run, not every possible use.

Frequently asked questions

Is Trama really free?

Yes. Trama is released under the MIT licence: no cost, subscription or per-seat limit, including for professional use.

Can I import my data from Excel or CSV?

Yes. You can paste from Excel and import CSV, TSV, TXT and XLSX files. A worksheet can stay linked to its CSV or TSV and refresh with File → Update from file…

Which systems does it run on?

64-bit Windows 10 and 11, with a screen of at least 1024 × 768 pixels. Extract the ZIP and run Trama.exe: no Python, account or activation key.

Can I use it for clinical or regulatory results?

Trama is a beta with partial coverage and no clinical validation or regulatory certification. Always verify the results: the limits are written here and in each version’s notes.
